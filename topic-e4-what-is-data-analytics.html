What is Data Analytics?

📎Module 01 — Introduction to Advanced Data Analytics · Giralyn R. Ongco, PhDTM
Data Analytics is the systematic process of collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and interpreting data to extract meaningful insights that support decision-making. Advanced analytics goes beyond basic reporting — using statistical models, AI, and automation on large, complex datasets to forecast and prescribe future outcomes.
🧩

Definition of Data Analytics

Data Analytics is the systematic process of collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and interpreting data to extract meaningful insights that support decision-making. It transforms raw data into actionable knowledge — enabling organizations to understand what has happened, why it happened, and what might happen next.
📊

Basic vs Advanced Data Analytics

Basic Analytics Advanced Analytics
Simple reports and summaries Predictive and prescriptive models
Describes past events Forecasts future outcomes
Manual or spreadsheet-based Uses algorithms, AI, and automation
Limited datasets Large, complex datasets
🚀

What Makes Data Analytics "Advanced"?

Advanced Data Analytics goes beyond asking "what happened?" and instead answers:
  • Why did it happen? (Diagnostic)
  • What will happen next? (Predictive)
  • What is the best action to take? (Prescriptive)
Key Characteristics of Advanced Analytics:
  • Uses statistical models and machine learning
  • Handles large and complex datasets
  • Employs automation and intelligent systems
  • Supports strategic and operational decisions

📋 Data vs. Information vs. Insight Foundations ›
Data is raw, unprocessed facts (e.g., numbers, text). Information is processed data with context (e.g., a sales report). Insight is actionable understanding derived from analysis (e.g., "Sales drop every Monday — adjust staffing"). Analytics is the process that moves from data → information → insight → decision.
💡 The goal of analytics is not just to produce reports, but to generate insights that drive decisions.
🤖 AI and Automation in Advanced Analytics Technology ›
Advanced analytics increasingly relies on AI-driven systems — systems where artificial intelligence actively influences or automates decision-making rather than merely reporting insights. These systems learn from data, adapt over time, and continuously optimize outcomes with minimal human intervention. The mantra is: AI-driven systems predict, decide, and act.
💡 Examples: Netflix recommendation engine, Google's ad targeting, credit scoring systems — all use advanced analytics + AI to make automated decisions.
🌐 Real-World Applications of Data Analytics Industry ›
Healthcare: Predicting patient readmission risk, drug interaction analysis. Finance: Fraud detection, credit risk modeling, algorithmic trading. Retail: Demand forecasting, customer segmentation, dynamic pricing. Government: Policy impact analysis, census data modeling, traffic optimization. Education: Student performance prediction, personalized learning pathways.
💡 Advanced analytics is cross-industry — any organization that generates data can leverage it for better decision-making.
