What is Narrow AI?

📎https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/artificial-intelligence/what-is-narrow-ai/
Narrow AI (Weak AI / ANI) is AI designed for one specific task or a limited set of tasks. It is the only form of AI that currently exists in the real world — from voice assistants to recommendation engines.
🔍

Definition of Narrow AI

Narrow AI (also called Weak AI or ANI) refers to AI systems designed and trained for a specific purpose or limited range of tasks. These systems operate under constrained, predefined conditions. All AI systems deployed today are Narrow AI — including ChatGPT (trained specifically for language tasks).
📱

Examples in Everyday Life

Narrow AI is everywhere: Voice Assistants — Siri, Alexa. Recommendation Systems — Netflix, Spotify, Amazon. Facial Recognition — Face ID, photo organization. Email Spam Filters. Search Engines — Google's ranking. Navigation — Google Maps. Medical Imaging AI — tumor detection. Fraud Detection — flagging unusual transactions.
ANI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)
The technical term for Narrow AI — AI systems trained for specific, constrained tasks.
Domain Specificity
Narrow AI only works within its predefined domain — a chess AI cannot drive a car.
Recommendation System
A Narrow AI that analyzes user behavior to suggest relevant content (Netflix, Spotify, Amazon).
Computer Vision
Narrow AI for image and video understanding — used in facial recognition, medical imaging, and autonomous vehicles.
⚖️

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: Superhuman accuracy in specific tasks (chess, Go, image classification). Processes data at machine speed. 24/7 availability. Limitations: Cannot generalize — a chess AI cannot do anything else. No understanding, awareness, or common sense. Brittle in edge cases and out-of-distribution scenarios.
🎮 Narrow AI in Gaming: AlphaGo & AlphaZero Case Study ›
DeepMind's AlphaGo (2016) mastered the ancient board game Go using deep neural networks and Monte Carlo Tree Search. AlphaZero (2017) learned chess, Go, and shogi from scratch via pure self-play reinforcement learning — no human games needed. These are landmark Narrow AI achievements demonstrating superhuman performance, but neither system can perform any task outside its game domain.
🏥 Narrow AI in Healthcare Real World ›
Medical Narrow AI: Radiology AI — Google's DeepMind AI detects breast cancer in mammograms with accuracy exceeding specialist radiologists. Drug Discovery — AlphaFold (DeepMind) solved protein structure prediction — a 50-year grand challenge in biology. Pathology — AI classifies cancer biopsies. Wearables — heart arrhythmia detection. Each is a highly specialized Narrow AI within a single medical domain.
